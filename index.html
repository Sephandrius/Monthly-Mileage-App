How to Use This Tool ▼

What is this tool for?

This tool processes employee mileage reimbursement spreadsheets and generates a summary report for payroll processing. The conversion happens entirely in your browser - no files are uploaded to any server.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your files: You have two options:
    • Drag and drop: Drag a folder or individual Excel files into the drop area.
    • Select files: Click the "Select Files" button and choose one or more Excel spreadsheets.
  2. Process the files: Click the "Process Files" button. The app will extract all mileage data.
  3. Review the summary: The app will show a summary of all employees, their total miles, and reimbursement amounts.
  4. Check for discrepancies: Any issues will be flagged with a "Discrepancy" indicator.
  5. View trip details: Click "View" next to any employee to see their individual trip records.
  6. Export the summary: Click "Export to Excel" to download a consolidated spreadsheet for payroll processing.

Expected File Format

Each employee's Excel spreadsheet should have:

  • Employee name in cell B2
  • Total miles in cell B3
  • IRS rate (as a decimal) in cell B4
  • Reimbursement amount in cell B5
  • Individual trips starting at row 7 with:
    • Column A: Date
    • Column B: Purpose
    • Column C: Description
    • Column D: Miles

Note: The app automatically finds the most common IRS rate across all spreadsheets and uses that for calculations. This helps catch outdated rates in some employees' files.
